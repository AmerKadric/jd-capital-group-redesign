Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Every relationship starts with a confidential, no-obligation conversation about where your business stands today and where it needs to go. There is no cost and no commitment.

What size businesses do you work with?

We work with founders, owners, and executive teams at growing businesses, from a single location to multi-location companies. Our experience includes helping Lady Jane’s grow from 53 to more than 100 locations.

What industries do you work in?

Our hands-on experience spans retail and personal services, hospitality, specialty coffee, real estate, and digital media. Because we focus on strategy, operations, and execution, the same approach carries across industries.

Do you work with businesses outside Michigan?

Yes. We are based in Birmingham, Michigan, and our work has reached beyond the state, including a waterfront development in Fort Lauderdale, Florida.

How are engagements structured and priced?

Every engagement is scoped to the business. After the first conversation, we outline the work, timeline, and fees in a clear proposal so you know exactly what to expect before anything begins.

How do you keep our information confidential?

Every engagement is strictly confidential, from the first conversation through final execution. We work with a select group of clients at a time, which lets us protect sensitive information and give each relationship real attention.
