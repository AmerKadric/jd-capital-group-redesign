Project 01 Retail & Salon Services

Lady Jane’s Haircuts for Men

Corporate Office & Flagship Salon — Project Lead

JD Capital Group led the design and build-out of Lady Jane’s corporate office and flagship salon, assembling and directing the full project team. The space was engineered around the brand’s service-centric positioning — bold graphics, high-energy color, and sightlines that keep operations visible throughout. We integrated an interactive LCD wall, a circular LED display, and a virtual check-in system, managing design, technology, and custom fabrication while drawing on established industry relationships to control cost.

Project 02 Specialty Coffee — Flagship Location

Birmingham Roast

Brand Launch — Concept to Completion

Engaged from inception, JD Capital Group built Birmingham Roast’s brand identity from the ground up — naming, logo, and a modern environment designed to deliver a true third-wave coffee experience. We stayed involved across every phase of the concept’s development, helping establish its presence in Birmingham’s specialty coffee market. As the flagship location, Birmingham Roast set the concept and brand standard that Royal Oak Roast and Plymouth Roast later built on.
