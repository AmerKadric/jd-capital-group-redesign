Foundations

Jesse Dhillon set out early to lead. He studied Finance at Michigan State University, where he served as Chief Financial Officer of the Associated Students of Michigan State University — overseeing a $1.5 million student fund and building the budgeting framework used to allocate resources across student government departments. In his senior year, he also helped manage $200,000 within a $5 million student-run investment portfolio, gaining early, hands-on exposure to institutional-level financial decision-making. After graduation, Jesse began his career at Deloitte Consulting, focused on Engagement Financial Management.

Leading Growth at Lady Jane’s Haircuts for Men

At 23, Jesse was invited to join Lady Jane’s Haircuts for Men — the nation’s largest corporately owned men’s hair salon brand — as Director of Finance. His impact was immediate: within two years he was promoted to Vice President, a role he holds today, overseeing key operations across a $30 million enterprise.

Working directly with the CEO, Jesse helped lead the company through two of its largest growth periods, during which Lady Jane’s expanded from 53 to 105 locations while supporting the largest workforce in company history. His scope of work touched nearly every part of the business — employee retention, accounting organization, IT improvement, business intelligence, inventory and cost reduction, branding, social media marketing, office and salon design, and cross-functional operations.

Birmingham Roast & Beyond

Alongside his work at Lady Jane’s, Jesse conceived and built Birmingham Roast, a third-wave coffee concept, from the ground up — owning the concept, design, business plan, and day-to-day operations from origination through launch. The concept has since grown into a small, multi-location coffee brand, expanding to Royal Oak Roast and Plymouth Roast, with additional locations in development.

Beyond the Office

Outside of work, Jesse spends his time with family and friends, and staying active through fitness and golf.

This breadth of experience — scaling an enterprise from the inside and building a brand from nothing — is the foundation JD Capital Group is built on.
